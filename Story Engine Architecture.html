The one rule the diagram exists to show

The computer counts. The AI writes.

Every figure in the finished deck comes out of a database query. The AI is handed finished, formatted numbers and asked only to say them well — and then a check pulls the numbers back out of what it wrote and compares them. A figure we did not send means the sentence is discarded and a plain template is used instead.

That check is what lets the deck go in front of the supplier's own analyst, which is the only test of this product that matters.
